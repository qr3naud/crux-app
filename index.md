# Crux

The AI finger-training coach for climbers.

Whether you're rehabbing a tweaked A2, chasing your first 7A, or breaking through a plateau — Crux turns hangboard sessions into structured, automatic progression.

## What it does

**Start without guesswork.** Tell Crux your grade and goal. Get a periodized plan in seconds — repeaters, max hangs, endurance, finger health. No spreadsheets.

**Rehab that doesn't feel like rehab.** Guided low-load progressions step up automatically as you tolerate more, with audio cues, rest timers, and AI nudges when you're pushing too hard.

**Level up when you're ready.** Periodized blocks adapt based on how sessions actually go. Conversational AI summarizes your last twelve weeks and flags what to push or pull back on.

**Live force when you want it.** Pair a compatible Bluetooth force gauge for real-time pull traces, in-zone glow, and percent-of-max benchmarks that follow your strength as it grows.

## Beta

## Compatibility

Crux works on its own for plan management, benchmark tracking, and AI coaching. For live force readouts, it pairs over standard Bluetooth Low Energy with compatible force-gauge accessories — including Tindeq Progressor (all models).
