# Crux

Train smarter on the hangboard.

Crux is an iOS app that pairs with [Tindeq Progressor](https://tindeq.com/) force gauges to give climbers a real-time hangboard training studio: live force traces, benchmarks that follow your strength, periodized training plans, and AI-tuned reads on your last twelve weeks.

The app is in beta. If you'd like access, [drop a note](mailto:reno.quentin@gmail.com).
