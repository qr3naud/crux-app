# Crux Support

For help with Crux — bug reports, billing questions, feature requests, beta access — email **[reno.quentin@gmail.com](mailto:reno.quentin@gmail.com)**.

## Common questions

**Crux Pro subscription.** Manage your subscription in iOS Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions. Cancellations take effect at the end of the current billing period; your training data stays.

**Restore purchases.** Open Crux, go to Settings → Restore Purchases. If your subscription was bought with the same Apple ID and is still active, it'll come back.

**Bluetooth pairing.** Make sure your force gauge is powered on and in pairing range. If Crux can't find it, force-quit and relaunch the app, then retry. Many devices can only be paired with one app at a time — disconnect from any other app first.

**Data export or deletion.** Email the address above with the subject "Data export" or "Data deletion" and we'll respond within a few business days.
